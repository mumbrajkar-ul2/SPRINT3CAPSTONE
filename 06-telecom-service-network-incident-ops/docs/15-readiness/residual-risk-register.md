## 1. How to read this file

Each row is one open risk. The columns are:

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Id | The id from the source file. `S08 D-03` is difference 3 of `docs/model-comparison/app-comparison.md`. `S05R D-11` is decision 11 of `docs/02-baseline/S05R_Decisions.md`. The two D numbers are different lists. |
| Finding and impact | What is true today, what harm follows, and the risk level. |
| Evidence | The file or test that shows it. |
| Confidence | High, Medium or Low. |
| Still open | The open question, or the part that no control covers. |
| Mitigated by | The control that reduces it today. "Nothing" means no control exists. |
| Accepted by | The person or role who accepted the risk, with the file that records it. "Unknown" means the packet names nobody. |

Worked example. Row `BL-12` says bandit finds 40 low-severity items. 2 are in `apps/api/services/policy.py` and 38 are in the second app. The "Mitigated by" cell says the scan stays on and no `# nosec` is allowed without a review. The "Accepted by" cell says Unknown, because nobody ruled on the findings.
